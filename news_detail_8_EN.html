Study Visit to Poland “Study of the Experience of the Republic of Poland Using the Example of UNI-LUBLIN (Lublin) in Implementing European Experience in Effective Public Administration and Training Relevant Personnel”

From September 27 to October 4, 2026, a Study Visit to Poland “Study of the Experience of the Republic of Poland Using the Example of UNI-LUBLIN (Lublin) in Implementing European Experience in Effective Public Administration and Training Relevant Personnel” (UNIWERSYTET MARII CURIE-SKLODOWSKIEJ) took place under the Erasmus+ project “Good Governance Practice: European Experience for Ukraine” ERASMUS-EDU-2025-CBHE-STRAND-1 (coordinator: Vitaliy STASHUK).
Project participants:

  • DRAGOMANOV UKRAINIAN STATE UNIVERSITY (coordinator)
  • BORYS GRINCHENKO KYIV METROPOLITAN UNIVERSITY
  • VASYL STEFANYK CARPATHIAN NATIONAL UNIVERSITY
  • NIZHYN MYKOLA GOGOL STATE UNIVERSITY
  • KRYVORIZKYJ NATIONALNYJ UNIVERSYTET
  • BALTIJAS STARPTAUTISKA AKADEMIJA
  • EKONOMICKA UNIVERZITA V BRATISLAVE
  • BERDYANSK STATE PEDAGOGICAL UNIVERSITY
  • INSTITUTE OF POLITICAL AND ENTHO NATIONAL STUDIES NAMED AFTER I F KURAS OF THE NATIONAL ACADEMY OF SCIENCES OF UKRAINE
  • UNIWERSYTET MARII CURIE-SKLODOWSKIEJ

During the stay, according to the program, meetings were held with the management and teachers of the UNIWERSYTET MARII CURIE-SKLODOWSKIEJ, involvement of the Study Visit participants in the university's ceremonial events, a visit to the cultural heritage of the city of Lublin; exchange of experience between the project participants on effective public administration and training of relevant personnel of the Republic of Poland, Slovakia, Latvia and Ukraine, and also the challenges faced and recommendations for expanding the impact of the project beyond its initial scope were considered.
